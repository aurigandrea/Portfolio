About Me

Hi! I'm Dr Andrea Kocsis, academic and web developer with a passion for making research accessible and engaging through technology.

I specialize in creating intuitive, playful, and accessible web applications that help academics and research teams share their work with the world. My approach combines modern web development practices with a deep understanding of academic needs.

I work closely with researchers to tell the story of their data—whether from the humanities, social sciences, or STEM. From interactive exhibition kiosks to responsive web apps, I'm experienced in tailoring solutions that bring research to engagement.

Whether it's building interactive data visualizations, creating engaging research websites, or developing tools for academic collaboration, I'm dedicated to crafting digital experiences that make complex information accessible to everyone.
